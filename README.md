# Matthew Wiecha

I'm an urban planner based in Brooklyn, New York, interested in transportation systems, public space, and the use of data to understand how cities work.

I hold a Master's in Urban Planning from Hunter College and a BA in Media Studies from Vassar College. My work includes transportation analysis, GIS mapping, policy research, and street design.

## Selected Work

- **Citi Bike Station Analysis** — Python-based analysis of bicycle and dock availability, station activity, and peak-period imbalances across New York City.
- **Grand Street Framework** — Transportation and public-realm planning for Grand Street in Brooklyn, developed through Hunter College's Urban Planning Studio.

## Tools & Skills

Python (pandas, Jupyter) · ArcGIS Pro · QGIS · Excel · Transportation Planning · Spatial Analysis
